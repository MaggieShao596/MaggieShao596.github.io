Ocean Pulse – 3rd Place, ML/AI Track @ DataHacks 2026

Team of 4 | Python, scikit-learn, Streamlit

An award-winning machine learning project predicting ecosystem health in the California Current using over 70 years of oceanographic data.

Background & Motivation

Ocean ecosystem health is influenced by complex environmental conditions. This project explores how oceanographic measurements can help classify ecosystem health and identify important predictors.

Data & Methods

  • Analyzed 286,819 oceanographic samples from the CalCOFI dataset (1949–2021).
  • Built a Random Forest classifier to predict three California Current ecosystem health states.
  • Conducted feature importance analysis to identify the most influential environmental variables.

Results & Takeaways

  • Achieved 98% classification accuracy across three ecosystem health states.
  • Identified phosphate and nitrate as the top two predictors of ecosystem health.
  • Developed an interactive Streamlit dashboard to communicate predictions and environmental feature trends.
  • Earned 3rd Place in the ML/AI Track at DataHacks 2026.

Tools: Python, scikit-learn, Random Forest, Feature Importance, Streamlit

California Real Estate Market Analysis

An analysis of California residential real estate data, exploring property features, pricing patterns, and house price prediction.

Background & Motivation

Housing prices vary widely across California. This project explores how location and property characteristics relate to those differences.

Data & Methods

  • Cleaned and prepared 178,925 property records.
  • Explored price distributions and relationships between features.
  • Used one-hot encoding and feature scaling to prepare model inputs.
  • Trained and evaluated a linear regression model using an 80/20 split.

Results & Takeaways

  • The linear regression model achieved an R² of 0.77.
  • Residual analysis showed greater error variation for higher-priced homes.
  • The project helped me practice data preparation, model evaluation, and communicating results.

Tools: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn

Sex Differences in Obesity Predictors

A health data analysis exploring BMI, calorie expenditure, and activity patterns across sex groups.

Background & Motivation

This project examines how health and activity measures relate to BMI, and whether those relationships differ across the groups represented in the dataset.

Data & Methods

  • Cleaned and organized health and activity data.
  • Performed exploratory analysis using Pandas and Seaborn.
  • Compared BMI, calorie expenditure, and activity measures by sex.
  • Created visualizations to explore patterns in the data.

Questions Explored

  • How do BMI and calorie expenditure vary across sex groups?
  • How strongly are workout calories associated with BMI?
  • Which other measures might help explain differences in BMI?

Tools: Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Data Visualization, Exploratory Data Analysis

A project exploring character dialogue and emotional patterns in the television series Friends.
